# 🎯 InterviewVault — Smarter Interview Preparation with AI

Most candidates prepare for interviews using random questions, generic answers, and blind job applications.  
**InterviewVault** was built to change that.

InterviewVault is an **AI-powered interview preparation platform** that helps candidates prepare based on their **resume, target job roles, and real interview expectations** — not guesswork.

**The goal is simple:**  
Help candidates prepare smarter, apply more confidently, and walk into interviews with clarity.

---

## 💡 Why InterviewVault?

During interview preparation, I noticed three common problems:

- Candidates practice **generic questions** unrelated to their resume or job role  
- People **apply randomly** without knowing which roles actually fit their skills  
- Resumes often get rejected by **ATS systems** before reaching recruiters  

InterviewVault addresses these problems using **Generative AI combined with structured workflows**.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### ✍️ Resume / JD-Based Q&A Generator
- Upload a **resume** or **job description**
- Automatically detects **technical and non-technical skills**
- Generates:
  - Technical interview questions based on actual skills
  - Behavioral and communication questions
- Each question includes:
  - A clear **~100-word answer**
  - 🔍 **Elaborate Answer** option
  - ✂️ **Shorten Answer** option for quick interviews

This helps users adapt answers based on **interview depth and time constraints**.

---

### 💼 Know Your Jobs (Focused Job Discovery)

Instead of applying everywhere, InterviewVault helps users apply **where they actually fit**.

- Analyzes resume skills
- Suggests:
  - Resume-based job roles
  - Domain-aligned opportunities
- Encourages **focused and targeted applications**, not random ones

---

### 📄 Resume Checker Module

Helps users improve resumes with **practical and actionable feedback**.

- 📊 **ATS Compatibility Score**
- 📋 **AI-based resume feedback** (content, clarity, tone)
- 🛠️ **Improved resume suggestions** tailored to role or domain

---

### 🧠 Interview Preparator Module

Structured preparation for **company-specific interviews**.

- Select **company name** and **job role**
- Get:
  - Common interview questions
  - Key skills to highlight
  - Company values and culture insights
  - Curated learning resources (articles, videos, blogs)
